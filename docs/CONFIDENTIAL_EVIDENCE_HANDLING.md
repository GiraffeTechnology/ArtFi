# Confidential evidence handling

The executed Artist cooperation agreement, custody and sellout-contingent donation undertaking,
CCHS custody-intake records, and associated identity, signature, witness and authority evidence are
classified as **confidential**.

The authoritative originals are retained at the CCHS office. They may be reviewed only in response
to a lawful process and only by authorized persons under controlled conditions. Review does not
authorize copying the documents into a networked workflow.

Source documents, scans, photographs, excerpts, or transcriptions must not be uploaded to any
public network environment. This prohibition includes Git repositories, public object storage,
websites, AI systems, model providers, prompt attachments, vector stores, evaluation datasets,
telemetry, and support portals. They must not be read, quoted, transcribed, summarized, or processed
by an AI workflow.

The sole project legal basis for the custody and post-sellout physical-artwork donation policy is
the declaration made by Michael YIP in his declared capacity as a CCHS Director and authorized
representative. The project may retain the declaration, its irreversible SHA-256 digest, and the
minimum non-content audit metadata needed to bind release policy. It must not require the
confidential source agreement or custody records to be uploaded to or stored in the repository.

This project rule does not purport to determine how a court or regulator would assess legal
capacity, formality, enforceability, title, or authority under applicable law.
