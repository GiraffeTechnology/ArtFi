艺术家简介
叶永润，香港东方书画院执行院长、香港书法家协会会员，研习书画逾六十载，尤以水墨虾著称。其书法师从吴子复，在传统中国画笔墨基础上融入对真实物象的长期观察，并借鉴近大远小的空间意识，着重表现虾体的透明质感、弹性与游动姿态。除虾之外，他亦创作竹石、花鸟、山水、熊猫和书法。其创作重视“形神兼备”，既延续中国书画的笔墨传统，也回应当代生活，期望让艺术走入家庭，在日常环境中带来审美愉悦与文化滋养。

Artist Biography
Ye Yong Run is Executive Director of the Hong Kong Oriental Academy of Painting and Calligraphy and a member of the Hong Kong Calligraphers Association. Active in Chinese ink painting and calligraphy for more than six decades, he is particularly recognized for his depictions of shrimp. He studied calligraphy with Wu Zifu and combines traditional brushwork with sustained observation of living subjects, using tonal and spatial variation to convey translucency, elasticity, and movement. His broader practice includes bamboo, flowers and birds, landscapes, pandas, and calligraphy. Across these subjects, Ye pursues a balance of physical likeness and expressive vitality while seeking to bring Chinese art into everyday life.
